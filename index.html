Nothing is wrong with you at 3 a.m.

Your body is doing what it was built to do. Cortisol starts climbing before dawn. Sleep gets lighter in the second half of the night. You surface.

Most people surface and drift back down. Some people surface and the mind starts working. Tomorrow's meeting. That email. The thing you said in 2019.

The problem is not the waking. It is what your nervous system does in the ninety seconds after.

That part is trainable. That is the whole protocol.

The protocol

Four evenings. Then it's yours.

  1. 01

    Sessions one to four

    You meet a certified instructor, live, with a small group. You learn SKY Breath Meditation properly — the rhythm, the count, what to do when the mind wanders off. Roughly 90 minutes each.

  2. 02

    The 3 a.m. practice

    A short breath you can do lying down, in the dark, without getting up. You learn this in session two.

  3. 03

    Every morning after

    Ten minutes. Sitting up, eyes closed. This is the part that changes the mornings.

You keep the technique. You keep the recordings. You can sit in on a group session anywhere in the country, any time, at no extra cost.

What do I actually get for $149?

Four live sessions with a certified instructor. The technique itself. Recordings you keep. And entry to group sessions, anywhere in the world, for as long as you want them.

How much time does this take?

Four evenings up front, about 90 minutes each. Then ten minutes a day. If ten minutes a day is genuinely not available, this isn't the right thing to buy.

I've tried meditation. It didn't work.

Most meditation asks you to sit still and do nothing with a mind that won't stop. If yours won't, that's a fair reason to quit.

This is breathing. Your hands are busy with a rhythm and a count. There's something to do. People who have failed at meditation tend to do fine here.

Is this religious?

No. There is nothing to believe and nothing to join. No chanting, no deity, no dress code. It's a breathing technique that came out of a tradition, taught by people who will not ask you what you think about God.
